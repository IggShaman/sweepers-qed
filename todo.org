- [ ] expore switching methods between primal and dual:
  - Changing only objective coefficients leaves the old basis primal feasible →
      primal simplex resumes immediately. This is your inner loop (maximize
      each cell in turn), and the default GLP_PRIMAL is already the right answer.
  - Changing bounds or right-hand sides leaves the old basis dual feasible →
      GLP_DUAL is the right choice. That's your outer loop: a cell gets uncovered,
      so you fix its column and tighten a clue row.
